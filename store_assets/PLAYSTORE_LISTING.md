## Full description — "About this app" (max 4000)
RK Classes is the official app of RK Classes for students, parents, teachers and the admin team. Attendance, lectures, test marks, fees, notes and updates are all in one simple, secure place — no more phone calls and paper registers.

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR STUDENTS & PARENTS
• Daily attendance with the teacher's name and a clear day-by-day record
• Lecture timetable with the time, subject, teacher and the CLASSROOM where the lecture is held
• Test marks subject by subject, class toppers and a full progress report with charts
• Fee receipts and fee status, shown the way the institute chooses
• Instant alerts for attendance, new lectures, results, fees and holidays
• Class notes and daily practice papers
• Birthday wishes from RK Classes
• Switch between children with one tap
• Your child's data is protected: screenshots and screen recording are blocked

👩‍🏫 FOR TEACHERS
• Take attendance in seconds — Present, Absent or Late, with one-tap call and WhatsApp to parents
• Add the lecture details once; your own attendance entry is recorded automatically
• See your lectures, the classroom you must go to, and your monthly totals
• Get notified when a lecture is assigned, changed or cancelled
• See your own attendance log, including any entry changed or deleted by the admin — with who did it and when
• Track your payments

🏫 FOR ADMIN & STAFF
• Dashboard: students, present and absent today, faculty, fees collected, balance and expenses at a glance
• Student records with search, batches, bulk batch change and discontinued students
• Attendance by class or batch, lecture by lecture, with holidays and absent lists
• Tests and marks entry with your own total for every test (20, 30, 50, 100…), top students and rankings
• Fee collection with receipts, fee structure, fee heads, additional charges and discounts agreed at admission
• Finance dashboard: class, batch and medium wise collection and balance
• Fee Follow-up: note who called whom, when, what was said, the reason, the amount promised and the next date — with a summary, full history and Excel export
• Expenses, teacher payments, salary types and payment modes
• Lecture schedule with weekly repeat, clash checks for teacher, class and room, and lecture summary reports
• CLASSROOMS: add all your rooms, pick one for every lecture, and see on a live board which room is free or engaged, until when, which lecture, teacher, class, batch and medium — plus the full past record
• Notes and papers for classes, parent accounts, users, roles and class representatives
• Export reports to Excel

🔐 SECURITY & PRIVACY
• Sign in with your FINGERPRINT — fast and safe
• Login Activity: every user sees on which phones their account was used; the admin can see everyone's
• The admin decides what each role, student and parent can see
• Class Representatives get access only to their own class
• Deleted records can be restored; every important change is recorded

🔔 ALWAYS IN TOUCH
Smart notifications keep parents, students and teachers updated the moment something happens.

📱 SIMPLE & FAST
A clean blue design, search everywhere (even inside dropdowns), and live updates.

Note: you need a login created by RK Classes. Parents and students sign in with the phone number registered with the institute; staff use the username given by the admin. Contact your institute if you need help.
